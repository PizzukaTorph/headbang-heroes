# Licensing & Rights Checklist

This document is an operational checklist, not legal advice.

## Rule zero

Nothing enters a public/commercial build without known provenance and permission compatible with the intended use.

## Music

For each song, record:
- song/composition title
- writers/composers
- performer/artist
- composition/publishing rights holder(s)
- master recording rights holder(s)
- who granted permission
- territory
- platforms
- term/duration
- commercial use
- videogame synchronization/use
- trailers/marketing/social clips
- livestream/user-generated-content implications where negotiated
- soundtrack/separate audio distribution, if any
- edits/loops/fades/stems permitted
- compensation: flat fee / royalty / revenue share / gratis
- credit requirements
- takedown/termination terms
- signed agreement location

Do not assume an artist controls every required right. Verify composition and master ownership.

## Preferred music acquisition

### Original/community commissions
Commission music with written terms covering the intended game and promotional uses.

### Underground band submissions
A future Underground Platform audition can be used for discovery, but submission alone must **not** constitute permission. Selected artists require explicit terms/agreements.

### Direct licensing
Small/underground bands are a preferred target when rights ownership is sufficiently clear.

## Real people and bands

Do not use a real musician's:
- name
- professional nickname
- likeness
- voice
- signature branding
- logos
- distinctive protected artwork

as a commercial character/boss/endorsement without reviewing and obtaining appropriate rights.

Fictional metal archetypes are the default.

## Third-party game assets

Track license/provenance for:
- Unity Asset Store packages
- fonts
- icons
- sound effects
- textures
- sprites
- animation
- plugins/SDKs
- code libraries

Keep notices/attribution where required.

## Repository hygiene

Because the repository may be public:
- do not commit commercial reference tracks
- do not commit unredistributable asset-store packages
- do not commit secret keys
- keep local/licensed content in ignored paths where appropriate
- document third-party dependencies separately

## Platform/compliance

Before release review:
- Apple/Google developer program requirements
- privacy policy
- analytics/ads consent requirements
- GDPR/ePrivacy implications where applicable
- age rating/content declarations
- account deletion requirements if accounts exist
- backend data retention/security
- user-generated content/moderation requirements if community features exist

## Licensing ledger

Create a structured licensing ledger before production content scales. Every production asset should map to a source/license/agreement record.
